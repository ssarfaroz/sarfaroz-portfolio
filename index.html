Case 01 · My product

Hunk answers the phone when a two-person HVAC crew can’t.

A voice-AI front desk for US home-service businesses. It picks up 24/7, checks real open slots in Google Calendar, books the visit and has the customer confirm by text.

Role
Founder, solo build
For
HVAC, plumbing, electrical and 9 more trades
Stack
Next.js, Supabase, Drizzle, Stripe, Twilio, Vapi
Status
Live at hunk.agency
  • Onboarding collects the trade, the US time zone and the service-area ZIP codes.
  • Texting follows the consent rules US carriers enforce: opt-in on the call, a YES/NO confirmation, STOP and HELP.

Case 02 · Client project

RCForYou compares car rentals in 13 countries and 7 languages.

A static site rebuilt every night by a Python generator, with a page for every country, city, airport, car class and rental condition, so a traveller lands on exactly what they searched for.

Role
Developer, contract
Stack
Python generator, PHP 8 API, vanilla JS
Scale
866 locations and 155 airports
Status
Live at rcforyou.com
  • An outage turned out to be a 172 MB JSON cache decoded on every request. A byte-offset index fixed it.
  • Strict Content-Security-Policy with no inline scripts, and analytics that load only after consent.
